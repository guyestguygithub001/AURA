# PRIVACY POLICY FOR AURA MARKETPLACE

**Last Updated: July 28, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how AURA ("we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and discloses information when you use our platform.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
- **Account Information**: Name, email address, password, phone number, and physical billing/shipping addresses.
- **Merchant Details**: Business registration numbers, banking data, and payout details required to configure Stripe Connect.
- **Transaction Details**: Products purchased, order value, timestamp, delivery address, and communication logs between Buyers and Merchants.
- **Technical Logs**: IP address, device fingerprints, browser configurations, and visual audit trail logs showing operational activities.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To facilitate order placement, payment capture, escrow processing, and shipping tracking.
- To maintain platform security, prevent fraudulent checkouts, and run transaction audits.
- To analyze platform metrics, optimize Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS), and boost storefront rendering speed.

## 3. Data Sharing & Third-Party Processors
We share data only with trusted service providers:
- **Stripe**: Handles processing and escrow allocation. Card data never touches AURA's servers.
- **Logistics APIs (FedEx/DHL/UPS)**: Address data is sent to generate labels and compute shipping costs.
- **Communication APIs (SendGrid/Twilio)**: Email and phone data are shared for transaction updates.

## 4. User Rights (GDPR & CCPA)
Under applicable regulations, you have the right to:
- Access, correct, or delete your personal data.
- Restrict processing or object to profiling.
- Request data portability.
To exercise these rights, please contact our support team.
