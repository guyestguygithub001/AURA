# DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (DPA)

**Last Updated: July 28, 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") governs the processing of personal data by AURA on behalf of Merchants utilizing our Platform services.

---

## 1. Scope & Roles
- **Merchant** acts as the **Data Controller** for Buyer information collected during the purchase of their goods.
- **AURA** acts as the **Data Processor** for such personal data, processing it solely to perform the platform services.

## 2. Processing Specifications
- **Subject Matter**: Providing e-commerce checkout, payment escrow, logistics integration, and messaging interfaces.
- **Duration**: The term of the Merchant's account plus statutory retention periods.
- **Categories of Data**: Buyer names, delivery addresses, emails, telephone numbers, and transaction details.

## 3. Data Processor Obligations
- **Instructions**: AURA shall process personal data only on documented instructions from the Merchant.
- **Confidentiality**: AURA ensures that personnel authorized to process data are bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
- **Security**: AURA implements technical and organizational measures to protect personal data (including encryption, firewalls, and regular security audit logs).
- **Sub-processors**: Merchants authorize the use of key sub-processors (e.g., Stripe, shipping carriers). AURA remains fully liable for sub-processor compliance.

## 4. Breach Notification
AURA shall notify the Merchant without undue delay (and in any case, within **48 hours**) after becoming aware of any accidental or unauthorized data breach.
