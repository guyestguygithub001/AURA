# REFUND POLICY FOR AURA MARKETPLACE

**Last Updated: July 28, 2026**

AURA enforces a strict, transparent Refund Policy to ensure transaction fairness and trust.

---

## 1. Escrow-Backed Buyer Protection
All customer purchases are held in an escrow account. The Merchant does not receive payment until:
- The logistics partner confirms package delivery.
- The **24-hour verification window** expires without a buyer dispute.

## 2. Refund Eligibility
Buyers are entitled to a full refund if:
- The product is not received within the guaranteed delivery timeline.
- The product arrives damaged, incorrect, or materially different from the listing description.

## 3. Return Shipping
- If a return is initiated due to merchant error (wrong item, defect), the Merchant is responsible for return shipping label costs.
- For buyer's remorse (e.g., wrong size ordered by buyer), return shipping fees are borne by the Buyer.

## 4. Refund Process
1. **Initiation**: Buyer clicks "Request Refund" in their transaction dashboard within the 24-hour verification window.
2. **Review**: The Merchant has 48 hours to accept the return or submit a counter-claim.
3. **Escrow Intervention**: If the parties cannot resolve the dispute, AURA support arbitrates the transaction, auditing delivery logs and product details to release or return funds.
