## 2. Multi-Party Escrow & Payment Processing
To protect both parties, AURA employs a strict transactional Escrow model:
- **Escrow Hold**: Payments from Buyers are captured and placed in a secure Escrow ledger.
- **Release Conditions**: Funds are released to the Merchant only upon successful delivery verification (e.g., DHL/FedEx integration logs marking the item as "Delivered") and the expiration of the 24-hour dispute window.
- **Platform Fee Split**: AURA deducts a standard **5% platform fee** from the total sale amount at the moment of payout settlement.
